# Idea: Lessons Learnt Action Item Register

## 1. Problem Statement

Lessons learnt are captured at post-delivery closure for each release, but the follow-up actions they generate are not tracked in a centralised register. An action identified after v1.6 may or may not have been addressed before v1.7 — there is no easy way to verify this. In practice, lessons learnt become historical documents that are read once and not revisited, meaning the same process failures tend to recur across cycles. This is a delivery process integrity failure.

## 2. Strategic Alignment

Section reference: PMO Lead §5 — Core Responsibility ("lessons learnt and process improvement")

Alignment rationale: The PMO Lead's mandate explicitly includes process improvement. A lessons learnt action register is the structural mechanism for ensuring process improvement is systematic rather than aspirational. Without it, the PMO Lead cannot demonstrate that post-cycle learnings are being actioned — which undermines the delivery process integrity that this role exists to protect.

## 3. Proposed Solution

Create `claude/governance/lessons_learnt_actions.md` — a Class 3 operational record updated after each release closure. Each entry contains: the source lesson (cycle and finding), the action item, the owner, the target cycle, and the resolution status. The register is reviewed as a standing agenda item at sprint kickoff. Closed items are retained as a historical record. The PMO Lead maintains the register and escalates unowned or overdue actions to the relevant role.

## 4. Expected Value

Increases the implementation rate of lessons learnt actions from effectively unmeasured to ≥80% closed by the target cycle. Eliminates recurrence of documented process failures. Provides an audit trail demonstrating that post-delivery improvement is real, not just documented.

## 5. Effort Estimate

- [x] Small — days to 1 week

Constraints or dependencies: Requires review of all prior lessons learnt documents to populate the initial backlog of outstanding actions.

## 6. Reversibility

- [x] Fully reversible — no lasting effects

Reasoning: A tracking document can be archived at any time with no structural impact.

## 7. What Would You Stop?

No view — leave to debate. This is a governance instrument, not a delivery item; it does not compete with product features.

## 8. Submitter Recommendation

Reasoning: v1.7 has just closed and generated lessons learnt. Now is the natural moment to capture any outstanding actions before they are forgotten.
