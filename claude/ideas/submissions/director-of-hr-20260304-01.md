# Idea: Agent Role Onboarding Guide

## 1. Problem Statement

When a new agent role is added to `claude/agents/`, there is no canonical process for onboarding the role into the governance system. Currently, a new role charter is created and the role is considered active — but there is no checklist of what governance documents the new role owner must read, no confirmation that they understand the authority model, and no orientation to the governance engines (sprint, roadmap, idea intake) in which they will participate. Roles are activated ad hoc, and governance literacy is assumed rather than verified.

## 2. Strategic Alignment

Section reference: claude/charter/team_charter.md — the team charter defines the roles and their accountability model

Alignment rationale: The governance model depends on every role owner understanding their authority boundaries, their read obligations, and the governance processes they participate in. Without a formal onboarding process, a new role owner may exercise authority they do not have, miss a mandatory input to a governance routine, or produce artefacts that do not meet the required standards. Onboarding governance is the HR mechanism for protecting the integrity of the authority model.

## 3. Proposed Solution

Create `docs/governance/agent_role_onboarding.md` — a Class 3 operational document defining: (1) required reading list for all roles (team_charter.md, document_lifecycle_guide.md, shared_standards.md, CLAUDE.md), (2) role-specific reading (the new role's own charter, the charters of roles they interact with closely), (3) the confirmation mechanism (a checklist the new role owner signs off), and (4) the orientation process for each governance engine (idea intake, roadmap, sprint). The PMO Lead maintains a record of completed onboardings.

## 4. Expected Value

Ensures governance literacy before authority is exercised. Reduces role-caused governance violations — such as authoring a canonical spec without authority or missing a mandatory gate. Measurable as: reduction in governance violations attributable to role-boundary misunderstandings.

## 5. Effort Estimate

## 6. Reversibility
