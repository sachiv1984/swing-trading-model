Not filed as a backlog item — this is a test-collection-order fragility, not a product defect, and the existing workaround pattern is already established; a broader fix (adding an explicit path setup to `conftest.py` once, for every test file) is a reasonable future cleanup but out of scope for this EPIC's own stories.
