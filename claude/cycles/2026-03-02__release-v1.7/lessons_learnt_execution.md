### EX-LL-01 — Execution engine not formally invoked; sprint required full reconstruction
**What happened:** The sprint was executed across multiple sessions without a formal invocation of `execution_prompt.md`. Code was delivered and branches were merged, but none of the execution governance artefacts were created (`execution_state.json`, `sprint_backlog.md`, `sprint_close.md`, `qa_evidence_EPIC-xx.md`). When delivery verification was invoked, its STEP -1 preflight failed because the required artefacts were absent. A reconstruction session was necessary before Phase 4 could proceed.

**Where in the routine:** STEP -1 (Preflight Gate) and all of Phase 3 (Sprint Execution).

**Root cause:** Process — the execution engine was not the entry point for the sprint. Work began directly without invoking `run sprint`, so the engine's artefact creation steps never ran.

**Impact:** Added a full reconstruction session before Phase 4 could begin. Reconstruction relied on MEMORY.md and git history rather than real-time engine output — increasing the risk of content gaps or approximations in the governance record.
