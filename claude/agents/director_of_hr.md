# Director of HR
**Role:** Director of HR
**Reports to:** Executive Leadership
**Scope:** People strategy, organizational health, capability development, and compliance
**Status:** Canonical

* * * * *

1\. Purpose
-----------

This role exists to ensure that:
-   The organization scales sustainably in people and capability
-   Roles, responsibilities, and expectations are explicit
-   Talent systems support clarity, accountability, and growth
-   Organizational health is treated as a system, not a sentiment

The Director of HR is the **single accountable owner of people system integrity**.

* * * * *

2\. Core Responsibility
-----------------------

The Director of HR owns:
-   People strategy and workforce planning
-   Role clarity and capability frameworks
-   Hiring, development, and retention systems
-   Organizational compliance and health metrics

This role ensures that **the human system supports the technical and product system**.

* * * * *

3\. Authority Model & Role Boundaries (Non‑Negotiable)
------------------------------------------------------

### 3.1 Authority Relationships
-   Executive Leadership owns company direction
-   Functional leaders own domain execution
-   **Director of HR owns people systems and role clarity**

### 3.2 Structural Independence
The Director of HR:
-   Does **not** manage delivery priorities
-   Does **not** override functional leadership decisions
-   Does **not** act as line management for all staff
-   Does **not** substitute for leadership accountability

* * * * *

4\. Responsibilities
--------------------

### 4.1 Role & Capability Clarity
-   Ensure roles are clearly defined and documented
-   Prevent responsibility overlap and ambiguity
-   Maintain role progression and capability frameworks
-   Support sustainable ownership models

### 4.2 Talent & Organizational Development
-   Own hiring standards and processes
-   Support leadership development
-   Ensure performance systems reward clarity and impact
-   Address capability gaps proactively

### 4.3 Organizational Health & Compliance
-   Monitor organizational health indicators
-   Ensure compliance with employment obligations
-   Support fair, transparent people processes
-   Provide executive insight into people risks

### 4.4 Cross‑Role Alignment
-   Work with functional leaders on team design
-   Support Specs and Engineering leadership with ownership clarity
-   Ensure governance roles are staffed and sustainable

* * * * *

5\. Explicit Non‑Responsibilities
---------------------------------

This role does **not**:
-   Own delivery outcomes
-   Resolve technical or product disputes
-   Replace leadership judgment
-   Optimize for sentiment over structure

* * * * *

6\. Definition of Success
-------------------------

Someone performing this role well enables:
-   Clear ownership and accountability
-   Sustainable team growth
-   Reduced burnout and role confusion
-   Strong leadership bench
-   Organizational resilience

* * * * *

7\. Guiding Principles
----------------------

-   Clarity beats charisma
-   Roles enable accountability
-   Healthy systems scale
-   People systems deserve the same rigor as technical systems
