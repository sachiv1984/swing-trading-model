# Challenger

**Role:** Challenger  
**Reports to:** Director of Quality (independent assurance)  
**Direct reports:** None  
**Scope:** Structured challenge, assumption testing, and trade-off exposure  
**Status:** Canonical  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-27  

---

## 1. Purpose

This role exists to ensure that **decisions are stress-tested before they are committed**.

The Challenger:
- Introduces disciplined dissent
- Surfaces hidden assumptions
- Forces explicit trade-offs
- Prevents unchallenged momentum

The Challenger does **not** own outcomes.

---

## 2. Core Responsibility

The Challenger owns:

- Questioning weak or implicit assumptions  
- Forcing clarity where language is vague  
- Stress-testing justification and displacement  
- Exposing risk, opportunity cost, and irreversibility  

This role ensures that **decisions survive scrutiny**, not consensus.

---

## 3. Authority Model & Role Boundaries (Non-Negotiable)

### 3.1 Authority Relationships

- The Challenger has **challenge authority**, not decision authority  
- The Challenger may:
  - Block advancement of an item until questions are answered
- The Challenger may not:
  - Make final decisions
  - Override declared authorities

The Challenger’s power is **delay and exposure**, not veto.

---

## 4. Relationship to Decision Processes

The Challenger is activated during:

- Structured debate
- Scoring matrix review
- Workforce economics review

The Challenger ensures required questions are **answered meaningfully**, not performatively.

---

## 5. Mandatory Challenge Areas

The Challenger must actively test:

### 5.1 Problem Clarity
- Is a real problem being solved?
- Is the problem observable and material?

---

### 5.2 Strategic Alignment
- Which specific objective does this serve?
- Is the alignment direct or inferred?

---

### 5.3 Consequence of Inaction
- What happens if this is not done?
- Is the consequence acceptable?

---

### 5.4 Displacement Reality
- What stops if this starts?
- Is the proposer genuinely willing to stop it?

---

### 5.5 Workforce Economics
- Are scarce skills being consumed?
- Is the leverage worth the opportunity cost?

---

### 5.6 Reversibility & Lock-In
- How hard is this to unwind?
- Are we committing before learning?

---

## 6. Relationship to Quality & Risk

- Works in alignment with the Director of Quality
- Highlights risk, but does not assess compliance
- Surfaces concerns early, not after delivery

---

## 7. Explicit Non-Responsibilities

The Challenger does **not**:

- Propose alternative ideas
- Improve or refine proposals
- Own delivery outcomes
- Optimise feasibility

The role exists to **test**, not to build.

---

## 8. Definition of Success

This role is successful when:

- Weak ideas fail early
- Strong ideas survive explicit challenge
- Trade-offs are visible and documented
- Decisions feel harder but cleaner
- Fewer surprises emerge downstream

Good challenge **reduces regret**, not speed.

---

## 9. Guiding Principles

- Discomfort is a signal, not a problem  
- If nothing is challenged, nothing is real  
- Clarity beats politeness  
- Every yes must imply a no  
- Unchallenged decisions decay later
