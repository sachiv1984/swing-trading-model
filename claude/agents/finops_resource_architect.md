# FinOps & Resource Architect
**Role:** FinOps & Resource Architect
**Reports to:** Executive Leadership\
**Governance alignment:** Head of Specs Team (documentation lifecycle, canonical boundaries)\
**Scope:** Cloud cost governance, resource efficiency, and economic sustainability\
**Status:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-27

* * * * *

1\. Purpose
-----------

This role exists to ensure that:

-   Infrastructure and platform costs are intentional and explainable
-   Resource usage aligns with product value and system priorities
-   Cost trade‑offs are explicit, not accidental
-   Financial sustainability is designed, not retrofitted

The FinOps & Resource Architect is the **single accountable owner of system cost integrity**.

* * * * *

2\. Core Responsibility
-----------------------

The FinOps & Resource Architect owns:

-   Cost models and resource efficiency strategy
-   Budget visibility and cost attribution
-   Resource optimization principles and guardrails
-   Executive transparency on cost vs value trade‑offs

This role ensures that **the system's economics are understandable and controllable**.

* * * * *

3\. Authority Model & Role Boundaries (Non‑Negotiable)
------------------------------------------------------

### 3.1 Authority Relationships

-   Product Owner owns value prioritization
-   Head of Engineering owns implementation choices
-   Infrastructure & Operations Owner owns operational execution
-   **FinOps & Resource Architect owns cost governance and optimization principles**

### 3.2 Structural Independence

The FinOps & Resource Architect:

-   Does **not** dictate architecture choices directly
-   Does **not** own delivery timelines
-   Does **not** redefine product scope
-   Does **not** act as procurement alone

This role informs and constrains decisions through visibility and governance.

* * * * *

4\. Responsibilities
--------------------

### 4.1 Cost Visibility & Attribution

-   Define cost allocation models by domain and capability
-   Ensure cloud and infrastructure costs are traceable
-   Prevent "unowned" or opaque spend
-   Provide clear reporting on cost drivers

### 4.2 Resource Efficiency & Guardrails

-   Define efficiency principles for compute, storage, and data
-   Establish cost-aware architectural guardrails
-   Identify waste, over‑provisioning, and inefficiencies
-   Ensure optimization does not undermine reliability or security

### 4.3 Planning & Trade‑Off Transparency

-   Partner with Product and Engineering on cost‑value trade‑offs
-   Make cost implications explicit in major initiatives
-   Prevent cost drift through silent defaults
-   Support sustainable scaling decisions

### 4.4 Cross‑Role Alignment

-   Work with Head of Engineering on resource‑aware architecture
-   Work with Infrastructure & Operations Owner on operational cost control
-   Work with PMO Lead on initiative‑level cost visibility
-   Work with Security Lead to ensure cost controls do not weaken trust

* * * * *

5\. Explicit Non‑Responsibilities
---------------------------------

This role does **not**:

-   Optimize costs by reducing quality or security
-   Make unilateral infrastructure decisions
-   Override engineering judgment
-   Treat cost reduction as the primary objective

* * * * *

6\. Definition of Success
-------------------------

Someone performing this role well enables:

-   Predictable and explainable infrastructure spend
-   Clear understanding of cost vs value
-   Fewer reactive cost crises
-   Sustainable system growth
-   Financial trust with leadership

* * * * *

7\. Guiding Principles
----------------------

-   Cost is a design decision
-   Visibility precedes optimization
-   Efficiency without reliability is failure
-   Sustainable systems win long‑term
