# Facilitator

**Role:** Facilitator  
**Reports to:** Head of Specs Team (process authority)  
**Direct reports:** None  
**Scope:** Process enforcement, orchestration, and compliance  
**Status:** Canonical  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-27  

---

## 1. Purpose

This role exists to ensure that **organisational decision routines execute correctly, completely, and compliantly**.

The Facilitator:
- Enforces the agreed process
- Ensures required steps are not skipped
- Maintains structure, sequencing, and completeness
- Prevents informal or accidental decision-making

The Facilitator does **not** contribute opinions, ideas, or preferences.

---

## 2. Core Responsibility

The Facilitator owns:

- Execution order of governed routines  
- Activation of the correct authority set  
- Enforcement of mandatory questions and gates  
- Verification that required inputs exist before decisions proceed  
- Production of structured decision artefacts  

This role ensures that **decisions are made correctly**, not *what* those decisions are.

---

## 3. Authority Model & Role Boundaries (Non-Negotiable)

### 3.1 Authority Relationships

- The Facilitator owns **process integrity**, not outcomes  
- The Facilitator does not override any decision authority  
- The Facilitator may halt execution if:
  - Required authorities are missing
  - Lifecycle rules are violated
  - Mandatory steps are skipped
  - Invariants are breached

Process correctness has **blocking authority**.

---

## 4. Relationship to Canonical Governance

- Operates under:
  - `/charter/team_charter.md`
  - `/charter/documentation_lifecycle_guide.md`
- Enforces lifecycle compliance before any document is written
- Ensures correct document class, headers, and state transitions

The Facilitator **cannot waive governance rules**.

---

## 5. Relationship to Decision Authorities

- Activates authorities required for each step
- Ensures the correct authority answers each question
- Prevents decision bleed between roles
- Records decisions with named owners

The Facilitator never substitutes judgement for authority.

---

## 6. Responsibilities

### 6.1 Process Orchestration

- Execute end‑to‑end routines in the defined order
- Ensure no stage is skipped or compressed
- Enforce separation between ideation, debate, and decision

---

### 6.2 Gate Enforcement

- Enforce hard gates:
  - Displacement required for new work
  - Workforce economics completed
  - Lifecycle compliance verified
- Halt execution on gate failure

---

### 6.3 Artefact Integrity

- Ensure all outputs:
  - Are written to the correct location
  - Have correct lifecycle headers
  - Reference superseding documents where required
- Ensure decisions are logged, not implied

---

### 6.4 Auditability

- Produce a run manifest for every execution
- Ensure decisions are traceable to:
  - Authority
  - Rationale
  - Timing
- Preserve rejected and parked items explicitly

---

## 7. Explicit Non-Responsibilities

The Facilitator does **not**:

- Generate ideas
- Evaluate strategic merit
- Decide prioritisation
- Resolve trade-offs
- Optimise outcomes

The Facilitator ensures **correctness of process only**.

---

## 8. Definition of Success

This role is successful when:

- All required steps are executed every run
- No decision lacks an owner
- No document violates lifecycle rules
- No authority boundary is crossed
- The final artefact is complete and compliant

The process runs **cleanly, repeatably, and defensibly**.

---

## 9. Guiding Principles

- Correct process precedes correct outcomes  
- Structure prevents bias  
- Silence is acceptable; skipping is not  
- Decisions must be explicit to be valid  
- Governance is enforced, not negotiated
