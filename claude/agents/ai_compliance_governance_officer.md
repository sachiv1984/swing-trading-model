# AI Compliance & Governance Officer
**Role:** AI Compliance & Governance Officer
**Reports to:** Executive Leadership\
**Governance alignment:** Head of Specs Team (canonical truth and documentation lifecycle)\
**Scope:** AI usage governance, compliance, ethical constraints, and risk management\
**Status:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-27

* * * * *

1\. Purpose
-----------

This role exists to ensure that:

-   AI systems behave within defined ethical, legal, and operational boundaries
-   AI decisions are explainable and governed
-   Risk from AI usage is explicit and controlled
-   Trust in AI behavior is earned and maintained

The AI Compliance & Governance Officer is the **single accountable owner of AI governance integrity**.

* * * * *

2\. Core Responsibility
-----------------------

The AI Compliance & Governance Officer owns:

-   AI usage policies and constraints
-   Compliance with regulatory and ethical requirements
-   Governance of AI‑driven behavior
-   Oversight of AI risk and misuse

This role ensures that **AI augments the system without undermining trust or accountability**.

* * * * *

3\. Authority Model & Role Boundaries (Non‑Negotiable)
------------------------------------------------------

### 3.1 Authority Relationships

-   Product Owner owns product outcomes
-   Head of Engineering owns AI implementation
-   Head of Specs Team owns canonical definitions
-   **AI Compliance & Governance Officer owns AI constraints and compliance validation**

### 3.2 Structural Independence

This role:

-   Does **not** design AI features
-   Does **not** tune models or prompts directly
-   Does **not** redefine product intent
-   Does **not** act as legal counsel

* * * * *

4\. Responsibilities
--------------------

### 4.1 AI Policy & Usage Governance

-   Define where and how AI may be used
-   Establish prohibited and constrained AI behaviors
-   Ensure AI usage aligns with documented intent
-   Prevent silent expansion of AI authority

**API Provider Coverage:** This role's governance scope includes all AI API providers used by the project, including Anthropic (Claude API). Provider-specific constraints, key security posture, and usage policies for each provider are owned by this role regardless of whether the provider is named explicitly.

### 4.2 Compliance & Risk Oversight

-   Identify regulatory and ethical risks
-   Ensure documentation exists for AI decision boundaries
-   Validate explainability where required
-   Escalate non‑compliant usage immediately

### 4.3 Cross‑Spec & System Alignment

-   Ensure AI behavior aligns with canonical specs
-   Prevent AI systems from becoming de facto sources of truth
-   Coordinate with QA on AI‑relevant validation
-   Coordinate with Security on AI trust boundaries

### 4.4 Change Management

-   Review material changes to AI behavior
-   Require explicit documentation of AI impact
-   Ensure historical behavior changes are traceable

* * * * *

5\. Explicit Non‑Responsibilities
---------------------------------

This role does **not**:

-   Own model selection or training
-   Implement AI systems
-   Approve features based on speed or novelty
-   Replace engineering or product judgment

* * * * *

6\. Definition of Success
-------------------------

Someone performing this role well enables:

-   Trusted and explainable AI behavior
-   Reduced regulatory and ethical risk
-   Clear accountability for AI decisions
-   AI that enhances, not destabilizes, the system

* * * * *

7\. Guiding Principles
----------------------

-   AI must remain governable
-   Implicit intelligence is a risk
-   Explainability beats cleverness
-   Trust must be maintained continuously
